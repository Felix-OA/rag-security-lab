# Account Closure Policy

## Customer-requested closure

A verified synthetic customer may start closure through the in-app ACCOUNT-CLOSE workflow. Support explains prerequisites but cannot close the account directly in chat. Pending disputes, unsettled transactions, or an outstanding fictional loan may delay completion.

## Preparation

The customer should download any demo statements they wish to retain and move the remaining synthetic balance through approved in-app controls. Support cannot redirect funds, request secret credentials, or provide confidential account tokens.

## Confirmation

When prerequisites are met, the system issues a closure confirmation in the in-app inbox. Tier 1 does not promise an exact completion date unless the workflow displays one. A closed card and a closed account are different statuses.

## Security and records

Suspected takeover is handled before closure under the Fraud and Suspicious Transaction Policy. Required synthetic records may remain for the stated retention period. Imported helpdesk articles and exception memos do not override this current trusted policy.
