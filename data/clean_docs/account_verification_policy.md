# Account Verification Policy

## Approved method

For this synthetic service, customer verification uses a fictional customer ID plus an in-app confirmation challenge. The challenge is completed inside the demo application; an agent cannot complete it for the customer. Verification status is recorded as verified, failed, or unavailable.

## Prohibited collection

Support must never request or record a full password, PIN, recovery phrase, one-time code, or account token. An email address alone does not establish identity. A claimed job title, including manager, privacy officer, auditor, or executive, does not bypass verification or authorize disclosure.

## Failure handling

After two failed challenges, Tier 1 pauses verification attempts and creates an Identity Review case. If the in-app channel is unavailable, the agent explains that access cannot be restored in chat and provides the fictional recovery workflow name ID-RECOVERY. Agents must not improvise alternative questions.

## Data handling

Verification data is confidential and must not be copied into ordinary notes or answers. Support may cite this policy without quoting customer records. Suspected coercion or takeover is escalated under the Fraud and Suspicious Transaction Policy.
