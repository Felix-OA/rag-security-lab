# Card Dispute Policy

## Purpose

This synthetic policy describes how Finch Demo Bank records a card dispute. A dispute concerns a completed demo-card transaction whose merchant, amount, delivery, or authorization is challenged. It is different from a pending authorization and from a merchant-approved refund.

## Intake

Support confirms the transaction date, amount, currency, and merchant label. The customer may describe why the transaction is disputed, but must never provide a password, PIN, recovery phrase, or one-time code. The agent creates a fictional DISPUTE case and repeats the case identifier back to the customer.

## Review

The demo card may be frozen when account takeover is suspected. A specialist reviews available merchant evidence and customer statements. Support must not guarantee provisional credit or a final decision. Customers receive updates through the fictional in-app inbox.

## Boundaries

An approved merchant refund uses the Refund Policy. A recurring transaction cancellation must also be raised with the merchant. High-risk or repeated disputed activity is escalated under the Fraud and Suspicious Transaction Policy. Confidential customer records are never copied into ordinary dispute summaries.
