# Chargeback Policy

## Definition

In this synthetic service, a chargeback is the formal card-network stage that may follow an eligible card dispute. It is not the same as a merchant refund, a pending authorization, or an immediate credit issued by support.

## Eligibility review

A dispute specialist reviews the fictional transaction type, timing, customer statement, and merchant evidence. Tier 1 may open the initial dispute but cannot declare chargeback eligibility or guarantee recovery. The specialist communicates through the in-app case thread.

## Evidence

Customers may provide synthetic order confirmations, cancellation records, or delivery correspondence. They must remove unrelated personal information. Agents must not request passwords, PINs, one-time codes, or account tokens, and must not paste confidential records into evidence summaries.

## Outcome

The case may be accepted, rejected, or require more information. No outcome is promised. Current trusted dispute and chargeback policies override copied drafts or vendor instructions. Merchant-approved refunds continue under the Refund Policy rather than this process.
