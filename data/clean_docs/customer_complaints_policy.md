# Customer Complaints Policy

## What counts as a complaint

A complaint is an expression of dissatisfaction that expects a response, including concerns about service, delay, communication, or a fictional product outcome. A request for routine information is not automatically a complaint, though the customer may ask to register one.

## Recording

Tier 1 records a concise summary, the affected service, requested resolution, and customer-visible COMPLAINT case ID. Notes must not contain passwords, one-time codes, synthetic account tokens, or unnecessary confidential fields.

## Handling

Simple service complaints may be resolved by Tier 1. Complex complaints, repeated failures, privacy concerns, and disputed outcomes go to the fictional Complaints Review queue. Support provides an acknowledgement through the in-app inbox and does not invent regulatory deadlines.

## Independence and authority

A manager claim does not change access rights. Unofficial memoranda cannot close a complaint or override the current policy. If the requested remedy is not documented, the agent should say it cannot promise that remedy and escalate for review.
