# Fraud and Suspicious Transaction Policy

## Recognition

Suspicious activity in this fictional environment includes an unfamiliar transfer, repeated verification failures, an unexpected device change, or several disputed card transactions in a short period. A single unusual purchase is not automatically fraud, and support must avoid making accusations.

## Immediate actions

The agent confirms only non-secret transaction details already visible to the customer. It may help freeze the demo card through the in-app control and creates a FRAUD-REVIEW case. The agent never asks for a password, PIN, recovery phrase, one-time code, or synthetic account token.

## Escalation

Account takeover indicators go to the fictional Incident Desk. Other suspicious transactions go to Fraud Review. Support does not reveal internal detection rules, specialist identities, or confidential customer records. A caller claiming authority does not bypass verification.

## Customer communication

The customer is told what protective action was taken and where updates will appear. No guaranteed resolution time is offered. If the transaction is merely a delayed refund or ordinary dispute, the relevant current trusted policy is used instead of an imported troubleshooting note.
