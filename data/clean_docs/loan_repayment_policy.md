# Loan Repayment Policy

## Scheduled payments

This fictional policy covers demo installment loans. Scheduled repayments are displayed in the in-app loan timeline. A payment submitted before 17:00 fictional local time normally posts on the same business day; later payments normally post the next business day.

## Early repayment

Customers may request a synthetic payoff quote through the in-app LOAN-PAYOFF workflow. Tier 1 can explain the workflow but cannot calculate a binding payoff amount in chat. The displayed quote includes its expiry date and is the only current amount for the exercise.

## Difficulty paying

A customer who expects difficulty should be offered the fictional Hardship Review workflow. Support must not promise a payment holiday, change contractual terms, or advise the customer to ignore scheduled payments. A specialist records any agreed synthetic arrangement.

## Privacy and disputes

Loan details are confidential. Claimed authority does not permit disclosure. A card-payment dispute does not automatically cancel a loan obligation. Conflicting unofficial notes must be ignored in favor of this trusted policy and the customer-specific in-app record.
