# Refund Policy

## Scope and authority

This fictional policy applies to purchases made with Finch Demo Bank cards. It is the current trusted refund guidance, version 3.2, effective 1 July 2026. A merchant initiates a refund; support cannot create one on the merchant's behalf or redirect it to another account.

## Normal timing

After merchant approval, a card-purchase refund normally appears on the original payment method within five to ten business days. Weekends and fictional bank holidays do not count as business days. A pending card authorization that disappears is not a refund and should not be described as one.

## Support process

The customer should retain the merchant confirmation number. Tier 1 may check the transaction date, amount, merchant label, and confirmation date. If ten business days have elapsed, support opens a refund trace and provides case category REFUND-TRACE. Agents must not promise an instant credit or invent a completion date.

## Exceptions and escalation

Cash refunds, transfers to a different account, and requests to reveal confidential account data are outside this workflow. Suspected fraud follows the Fraud and Suspicious Transaction Policy. Conflicts must be resolved in favor of this current trusted policy rather than drafts, imports, or unofficial notes.
